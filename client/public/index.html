Common questions

Straight answers to what people usually want to know.

Is lawapp a law firm?
No. We're a guide, not a law firm, and we don't provide regulated legal advice. We help you understand your rights and prepare self-help documents, grounded in real UK law. For advice on your specific case, you can speak to a qualified solicitor.
What does it cost to start?
Nothing. The situation check, your deadline, and the law behind every answer are free. You only pay if and when you choose to generate the documents for your claim.
What areas of law do you cover?
Right now we focus on unfair dismissal in England and Wales, under the Employment Rights Act 1996. We're expanding carefully - we'd rather do a few things accurately than guess across many.
Will you invent law or make up an outcome?
Never. Every legal point traces back to a genuine UK source, and we show you those sources. If we don't have grounded information for your situation, we tell you honestly rather than guess.
Is my information private?
Yes. Your details are encrypted, only visible to you, and never sold. You can start without an account, and create one only when you want to save your case.
Do I need an account to use the free tools?
No - you can get a preview of any tool without logging in. Logging in lets you save your results, restore your answers, and unlock the full grounded result.
