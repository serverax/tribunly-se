PIPELINE WIRING
---------------
_write_audit_log() added to backend/engine/pipeline.py.
Called at Stage 6b, after governance decision, before returning the response.
Runs regardless of governance pass or fail.
Fails silently (logs error) rather than crashing user-facing response.

Audit payload (what IS stored):
  - fact_snapshot_hash: SHA256 of de-identified facts (fingerprint only)
  - rules_used: [{rule_key, authority_ref}]  -  no monetary values, no descriptions
  - retrieval_bundle: {rules_count, authorities_count, authority_types}
  - model_provider: class name (StubReasoningModel, ClaudeReasoningModel, etc.)
  - model_name: explicit model ID (claude-haiku-4-5-20251001)
  - boundary_log: {fields_stripped, fields_passed, pii_in_output}
  - grounding_score, confidence_score (float)
  - governance_result: {passes, failure_reason}
  - output_version: "1.0"

Audit payload (what is NOT stored):
  - Raw user facts
  - Personal data: names, employers, addresses, DOB, emails, phone numbers,
    national insurance numbers, medical details
  - Free-text case narrative (reasoning_summary, key_weaknesses)
  - Assessment value estimates as free text
  - Any field beginning with "_" (deterministic context injection)
